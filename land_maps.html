Wead Ohio Land Atlas

This working atlas keeps the two Ohio family landscapes separate: Ebenezer Wead in the Dayton, Van Buren Township, Oakwood, and Kettering area; and James Wead in the Massie’s Creek and Stevenson Cemetery area of Greene County. The first version shows research zones rather than pretending that unverified circles are deed boundaries.
