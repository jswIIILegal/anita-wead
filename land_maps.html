Wead Ohio Land Atlas

This working atlas keeps the two Ohio family landscapes separate: Ebenezer Wead in the Dayton and Van Buren Township area, and James Wead in the Massie’s Creek area of Greene County. It also separates Ebenezer’s still-unlocated personal farm from the later, better-documented Oakwood holdings of John and Robert Wead and their heirs.

Dayton and Oakwood reconstruction

  • Keep Ebenezer’s personal farm as an unresolved Van Buren Township research area.
  • Map the Oakwood Wead-family tract separately as a candidate area tied to John and Robert Wead and later heirs.
  • Use the 1872 J. S. Wade land record and the 1901 Wade Road survey as primary road-and-land controls.
  • Georeference Library of Congress Olmsted Job 6720, the 1919–1921 “Wade tract / Woodside” file.
  • Treat historic Wead Road as the road later renamed Patterson Road; Dorothy Lane and Patterson Boulevard are separate naming episodes.
  • Keep Babbitt’s Wead Street, later Lawnview Avenue, as a separate historical feature.
