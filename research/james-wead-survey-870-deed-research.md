# James Wead Greene County Deed Research: William Darke Survey No. 870

## Purpose

This memo preserves the actionable findings from the July 2026 deed-research report on the former **J. Wade / James Wead parcel in William Darke Survey No. 870**, Greene County, Ohio. It should be used with `ohio-land-map-sources.md` and `James_Wead.html` when reconstructing the Greene County land map.

The important result is not a final deed citation yet. The important result is that the search has been narrowed to a specific recorder-index workflow for the period after James Wead's death.

## Bottom line

- The former **J. Wade** parcel in **William Darke Survey No. 870** should not be treated as having disappeared. The working interpretation is that the map label disappeared because **title changed after James Wead's death**, likely through an estate, devisee, or heir conveyance.
- The decisive record should be a **post-1873 deed or probate-related conveyance** recorded in Greene County land records.
- Greene County's modern public web search is not expected to solve this period. The report states that the public online search starts in 1984 and hosted historical index books begin only in 1919.
- The controlling access point for 1873-1880 is the Greene County Recorder deed catalog on FamilySearch, especially:
  - **General index to deeds - direct 1803-1965; reverse 1803-1965; deeds 1798-1901**
  - **Direct index volume 6-7 (1873-1889)**
  - **Film 461683 / DGS 8330821**
- The grantee and exact deed book/page remain unresolved.

## Search target

The next step is a targeted pull from the Greene County deed indexes, not broad discovery.

### Primary deed-index volume

Search **direct index volume 6-7 (1873-1889), DGS 8330821**, then use the corresponding reverse index and deed books in the same Greene County Recorder collection.

### Search years

Start with **1873-1880**, with special attention to the first few years after James Wead's death.

### Search headings

Search under each of these headings:

- **Stevenson, John** — if John Stevenson conveyed as administrator with the will annexed.
- **Wead / Wade / Weed / Waid / Wood** — surname variants and possible indexing forms.
- **Robert E. Wead**
- **Joseph S. Wead**
- **John S. Wead**
- **Grove** — for Elizabeth Grove and husband.
- **Eckman** — for Ellen Joanna Eckman and husband.

## Highest-probability transfer models

1. **Administrator's deed:** John Stevenson, administrator with the will annexed of James Wead, conveyed the land to an unknown grantee.
2. **Single devisee deed:** all relevant heirs/devisees and spouses conveyed together to one purchaser.
3. **Separate one-fifth-interest deeds:** heirs conveyed individual undivided shares that were later consolidated by one buyer.

The report's key warning is that the post-death conveyance is more likely to be indexed under the party conveying the land than under James Wead alone. A search only under James Wead / James Wade may miss the controlling deed.
